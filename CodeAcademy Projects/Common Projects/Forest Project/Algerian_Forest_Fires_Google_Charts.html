Key insight: Temperature reduces humidity at the same rate in both regions (parallel lines). Once a fire occurs, the temperature–FFMC relationship flattens dramatically — an interaction is required. FFMC vs humidity is curved (quadratic), not linear.

Humidity vs Temperature by Region

Parallel slopes: each +1 °C lowers humidity by ~2.4 pp. Sidi Bel-abbes sits ~7 pp lower.

FFMC vs Temperature by Fire

Interaction: No-Fire slope ≈ +2.45; Fire slope ≈ +0.56. Different slopes are required.

FFMC vs Humidity — Quadratic Fit

Observed points + quadratic curve (FFMC = 77.63 + 0.75·humid − 0.0114·humid²). Protective effect of humidity strengthens at higher levels.

Mean Values by Fire Status

Key Correlations
